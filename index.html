The founding statement

Preamble

The Progressive Conservatives are conservatives who are progressive. We conserve the values of everyone, and we progress them all collectively into the future. We have the hard conversations no one else is willing to have, and we already have answers to some of the most circular debates.

01Platform

The hard conversations

We do not outsource circular debates to slogans. We answer them.

02Platform

The body

Abortion is left between the woman and God. The husband gets a say, but if she abuses that, they are not spiritually compatible anyway. Let her face judgment from God, not from man. Everyone should have full anonymity over their vessel — and with that, full responsibility for it.

03Platform

Currency

Currency is abolished. That answer is taken from IntelOasis (After Money / the Commons): money does not become a score; it stops existing as a concept. Side systems give people the house and the baseline. What matters is what you produce, and AI will find a use for everything.

04Platform

What we conserve

The values of everyone — not one faction’s values at the expense of the rest.

05Platform

What we progress

Those values, together, into the future. Conservation without a future is nostalgia. Progress without conservation is erasure.
